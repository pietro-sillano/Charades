How to play
  1. Teams take turns. One player per turn acts while their team guesses, or wears the phone on their forehead in tilt mode while the team acts.
  2. No talking, no mouthing, no pointing at objects in the room.
  3. Classic signs: hold up fingers for the number of words, tug your ear for "sounds like", crank an old camera for a film, open your palms for a book, sing silently for a song.
  4. Score a point for each word guessed before the timer runs out. After each turn you can fix any mistakes before scores are added.
